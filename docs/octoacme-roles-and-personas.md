# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security & Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure project work meets required controls, privacy requirements, and risk standards without slowing delivery unnecessarily.

### Responsibilities
- Review features and releases for security and compliance impact
- Define secure-by-default patterns and required controls
- Coordinate remediation for vulnerabilities and policy exceptions
- Partner with PMs and engineering leads on risk prioritization and escalation
- Support launch readiness and audit-related documentation

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Ensure decisions balance speed, safety, and policy requirements
- Provide clear escalation paths for high-risk issues

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and risk assessments
- Dependency planning with engineering and PM stakeholders
- Incident and remediation updates during production issues

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review architecture and implementation risks
- Partners with Product Managers to assess release trade-offs and compliance constraints
- Coordinates with Project Managers to track remediation work and go/no-go decisions

---

## QA & Release Quality Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Release Quality Leads define and uphold quality gates throughout delivery, with special attention to release readiness and regression prevention.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality criteria, and regression coverage approach
- Coordinate manual testing, validation, and acceptance checks with stakeholders
- Review release readiness against project outcomes and critical workflows
- Partner with engineering and PMs to resolve defects and confirm production readiness
- Track issues that affect customer confidence or operational quality

### Goals
- Improve confidence in releases and reduce customer-facing defects
- Create predictable quality checks across planning, execution, and deployment
- Align testing effort to the highest-risk scenarios and business priorities

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and status updates during sprint and release reviews
- Quality gate discussions with Product Managers and Developers
- Release readiness signoff and post-release follow-up

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers on test coverage and defect triage
- Collaborates with Project Managers to sequence QA activities with milestones and releases
- Supports Product Managers by validating that outcomes and acceptance criteria are truly met

---

## Technical Architect / Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects and Engineering Leads provide design direction, technical consistency, and system-level thinking across project work.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture decisions, trade-offs, and technical standards
- Identify cross-team dependencies and integration risks early
- Review design proposals for scalability, maintainability, and operational fit
- Support technical prioritization and delivery planning with Product and Project leads
- Facilitate alignment on standards, patterns, and technical guardrails

### Goals
- Promote healthy system design and long-term maintainability
- Reduce rework caused by inconsistent technical decisions
- Balance near-term delivery goals with long-term platform health

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical dependency planning with engineering teams and stakeholders
- Cross-project alignment on standards or platform impacts

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers on implementation decisions and alternative approaches
- Partners with Product Managers on feasibility, risks, and sequencing trade-offs
- Works with Project Managers to surface technical dependencies that may affect dates or milestones

---

## Customer Success / Enablement Partner

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Enablement Partners connect delivery work to customer experience, adoption needs, and business readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Translate customer feedback into actionable product and delivery priorities
- Support rollout communication, training, and enablement activities
- Monitor adoption indicators and highlight operational or usability issues
- Coordinate with Product and Project teams on stakeholder readiness and release messaging
- Capture lessons learned from customer usage and support interactions

### Goals
- Improve customer outcome realization after launch
- Reduce friction between delivery teams and adoption/support stakeholders
- Ensure product changes match customer needs and operational readiness

### Typical Communication
- Feedback loops with customer-facing teams and stakeholders
- Release readiness and adoption support updates
- Cross-functional review of usage, support trends, and lessons learned

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds customer insight into Product Manager prioritization and roadmap decisions
- Collaborates with Project Managers on communications, rollout timing, and stakeholder alignment
- Partners with Developers and QA leads to validate whether changes address real user needs

---

## Platform / DevOps Owner

### Role Summary
Platform and DevOps Owners provide the operational foundations needed for reliable delivery, deployment, and ongoing system health.

### Responsibilities
- Manage environments, pipelines, deployment mechanisms, and automation
- Own service reliability, observability, and operational readiness checks
- Coordinate platform dependencies and infrastructure changes with engineering teams
- Support release execution, rollback planning, and incident response readiness
- Help ensure the team can deliver safely and repeatedly at scale

### Goals
- Improve deployment confidence and system reliability
- Reduce manual operational friction and release risk
- Support a consistent, scalable delivery engine for the project

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment planning
- Infrastructure or environment dependency updates
- Incident response and operational health discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to ensure environments and pipelines support delivery needs
- Partners with Project Managers on deployment windows, rollback planning, and release sequencing
- Supports Product Managers and Security leads by providing operational evidence needed for release confidence and risk management
